# Killer

A knockout game for 2–8 players. Everyone owns one number and a stack of lives. First you arm yourself into a **killer**, then you hunt the others — hit *their* number to knock lives off them. Run someone down to zero and they're out. Last player standing wins.

## Getting your number

- **Throw for it** (default) — each player in turn throws until they land on a free number (1–20); that number is theirs. Bulls and already-taken numbers don't count, so keep throwing.
- **Random** — every player is dealt a different number automatically and play starts straight away.

## Becoming a killer

You can't take anyone's lives until you're armed. How you arm depends on the **mode**:

- **Standard** — hit your **own** number with any dart to gain lives: a single is +1, a double +2, a treble +3, up to the lives cap. Reach the cap and you're a killer.
- **Double Trouble** — hit **your double** to become a killer. Only doubles do anything.
- **Treble Trouble** — the same, but you arm on **your treble**.

## Taking lives

Once you're a killer, hit an **opponent's** number to knock their lives down:

- **Standard** — a single / double / treble removes **1 / 2 / 3** lives.
- **Double / Treble Trouble** — each qualifying hit removes **one** life.

Drop a player to **0 lives** and they're **out** for the rest of the game.

## Options

- **Mode** — Standard (count up on any ring), Double Trouble (doubles only), or Treble Trouble (trebles only).
- **Numbers** — throw for your number, or have them dealt at random.
- **Lives** — how many lives each player starts with (and, in Standard, the cap you build up to). Default 3.
- **Self-kill** — when on, once you're a killer, hitting your **own** number costs you a life — miss carefully, you can knock yourself out. When off, your own number is safe.
- **Straight off** — when on, everyone starts already a killer, skipping the arming phase — you can take lives from the first dart.

The **last player standing wins**.
