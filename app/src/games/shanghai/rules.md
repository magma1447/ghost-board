# Shanghai

Each round targets one number, in order: round 1 is the 1, round 2 the 2, and so on. Only that round's number scores — a single counts once, a double twice, a treble three times. The highest total after the final round wins.

The catch: hit the single, double **and** treble of the round's number in a single turn — a "Shanghai" — and you win instantly, whatever the score.

## How to play

- Each round, everyone aims at that round's number (round 1 → the 1, round 2 → the 2, and so on).
- A single scores the number once, a double scores it twice, and a treble three times.
- Darts on any other number score nothing.
- After the last round, the highest total wins.
- A "Shanghai" (single + double + treble of the number in one turn) wins on the spot.

## Options

- **Rounds** — how many numbers you march through (7 is the classic short game, 20 the full game).
- **Shanghai instant win** — whether the single + double + treble-in-one-turn instant win is active.
- **On a tie** — end level scores as a draw, or play sudden-death rounds until someone leads.
