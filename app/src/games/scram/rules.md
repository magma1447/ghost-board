# Scram

Scram is a two-player game played in two halves. In each half the players take opposite roles: one is the **stopper** and the other is the **scorer**. Swap roles for the second half. Whoever has the most points across both halves wins.

The stopper races to close every number. The scorer piles up points on every number the stopper hasn't closed yet — so the stopper wants to shut numbers down fast, and the scorer wants to milk them first.

## How to play

- The numbers in play are 20 down to 15 plus the bull (or whichever set you pick).
- **Stopper:** hit each number three times to close it — a single counts once, a double twice, a treble three times (outer bull once, inner bull twice). Extra hits on a closed number do nothing.
- **Scorer:** every hit on a number that is still open scores that number's value (bull is worth 25), multiplied by single/double/treble. Once the stopper shuts a number, it stops paying out.
- The half ends the moment the stopper closes the **last** open number.
- Then the roles swap, every number reopens, and you play the second half.
- After both halves, the higher total wins.

## Options

- **Numbers** — which numbers are in play: the classic 15–20 + bull, a bull-free 14–20, or a random set (with or without the bull).
- **On a tie** — if the scores end level, call it a draw, or play sudden death: another pair of halves on **3 random numbers** from the set (a fresh 3 each time) until someone leads.
