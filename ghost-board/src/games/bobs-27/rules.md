# Bob's 27

A doubles-accuracy drill. Everyone starts on **27 points** and works through every double in order. Hit the round's double and it pays out; miss it entirely and it costs you. Highest total after the card wins.

## How to play

- Everyone starts with a score of **27**.
- Each round names one double to aim at, in order: **D1, D2, D3, … D20, then the double bull**.
- On your turn (3 darts), every dart that lands on the round's double adds that double's value — D1 is worth 2, D6 worth 12, D20 worth 40, the double bull 50.
- Land at least one dart on the target and you keep everything you scored this turn.
- Miss the target with **all three** darts and that double's value is **subtracted** from your total.
- After the last round (the double bull), the highest total wins. A flawless card — every double, three darts each — tops out at **1437**.

## Options

- **Elimination** — when on, dropping to **0 or below** knocks you out for the rest of the game; the last player standing wins (or, if the card completes with several still in, the highest total). Off, scores can go negative and everyone plays the whole card.
- **Final bull** — finish on the **double bull (50)** only (the authentic, hard version), or **any bull** — which lets the outer 25 count as a hit and softens the miss to −25.
- **On a tie** — end level totals as a draw, or play sudden-death double-bull rounds until someone leads.
