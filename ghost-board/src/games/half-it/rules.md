# Half It

Every round has one target. Hit it and you bank points; whiff it entirely and it costs you — your whole total is cut in half. The highest total after the final round wins.

## How to play

- Each round names a single target that everyone aims at, in this fixed order:
  **20, 16, any double, 17, 18, any treble, 19, 20, bull**.
- On your turn (3 darts), every dart that hits the round's target adds its face value to your running total — a treble 20 is worth 60, a double 16 worth 32, the bull 25 (double bull 50).
- Land at least one dart on the target and you keep everything you scored this turn.
- Miss the target with **all three** darts and your total is halved, rounded down (a total of 85 becomes 42).
- After the last round (the bull), the highest total wins.

## Options

- **Starting score** — the total everyone begins with (usually 0).
- **On a tie** — end level totals as a draw, or play sudden-death bull-off rounds until someone leads.
