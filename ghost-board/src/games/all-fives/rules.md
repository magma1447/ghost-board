# All Fives

Each turn, add up your three darts. If the total is a **multiple of 5**, you score the total divided by 5 — so a turn of 65 scores **13**. If the total isn't a multiple of 5, the turn scores **nothing**.

First to reach the **target** (51 by default) wins.

## Tips

- The multiples of 5 on the board — 5, 10, 15, 20 and the bull — always keep your total on a five, whatever ring you hit. **Treble 20 (60)** is the biggest safe score: three of them is 180 = 36.
- If a dart knocks your total off a five, use your remaining dart to nudge it back — a single that makes the running total divisible by 5 rescues the turn. (The board lights the numbers that do it.)

## Options

- **Target** — the score to reach. By default you must land on it **exactly**; overshooting busts the turn (it scores nothing).
- **Allow overshoot** — reach or pass the target to win, instead of finishing exactly.
- **Bull scoring** — 25 outer / 50 inner (standard), or 50 / 50.
